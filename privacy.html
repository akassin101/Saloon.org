Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Saloon ("we," "us," or "our") operates saloon.org. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

Account information. When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a photo ID. Your name and email are stored on our servers. Your photo ID is used solely to verify your real identity and is not retained after verification is complete.

Google Sign-In. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. We do not receive your Google password or payment information.

Content you post. Conversations, comments, and profile information you submit are stored on our servers and visible to other users.

Usage data. We collect basic server logs (IP address, request times) for security and debugging purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to operate Saloon — to authenticate you, display your profile, and facilitate conversations. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising.

Identity Verification

Saloon requires real-name participation. Your submitted ID is reviewed by an administrator to confirm your identity matches your registered name. IDs are handled confidentially and are not shared with other users.

Data Retention

Your account data is retained as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account by contacting us. Content you have posted may remain visible after deletion at our discretion.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

Google Identity Services — for optional Google Sign-In. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Resend — for transactional email delivery (verification and password reset emails).

Railway — for server hosting and data storage.

Netlify — for frontend hosting.

Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to keep you signed in between sessions. We do not use third-party tracking cookies.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at admin@saloon.org.

Children

Saloon is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will note the date of the last update at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email admin@saloon.org.
